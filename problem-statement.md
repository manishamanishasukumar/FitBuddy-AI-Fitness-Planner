In today's busy life, 80% of people want to stay fit but cannot afford a personal trainer which costs Rs. 5000 - 15000 per month. They depend on generic YouTube videos which are not suitable for everyone.
- A 20-year-old and a 50-year-old cannot do same workout.
- A person with Knee Pain, Diabetes, BP cannot do heavy workouts.
- People have only 30-45 mins per day, need time-based plan.
So, there is a need for a low-cost, personalized, AI-based fitness planner.
