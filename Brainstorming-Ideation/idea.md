*Problem Identified:**
Most people cannot afford a personal trainer. YouTube workouts are generic and not suitable for everyone's age, weight, health issues (knee pain, diabetes), and time.

**Proposed Solution:**
FitBuddy is a web app where user enters:
- Personal: age, gender, weight, height, goal (Weight Loss / Muscle Gain / Stay Fit)
- Lifestyle: daily available time, diet preference
- Health: existing conditions
Gemini AI will generate a personalized 7-day workout plan with exercises, sets, reps, and diet tips.

**Innovation:**
User can give feedback like "Day 3 is too hard, I have knee pain" and AI will regenerate that day instantly.
